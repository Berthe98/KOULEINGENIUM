FAQ

Common questions

How accurate is the estimate?

With an STL file or slicer numbers it is usually close. If you only give dimensions it is a rougher guide. In every case we confirm the final price before printing starts.

I don't have a 3D file. Can you still help?

Yes. Send us a sketch, photos or measurements on WhatsApp and we can discuss modelling the part for you.

Is my design kept private?

The estimator measures your file inside your browser and never uploads it. If you send us the file to print, we use it only for your order.

How long does printing take?

It depends on size and quantity. Small parts can be ready within a day; larger jobs take longer. Choose “Rush order” if you are in a hurry and we will confirm the delay.

How do I pay?

We agree the final price and payment method with you on WhatsApp before we start printing.
